## Conventions

- `<argument>` is required, `[argument]` optional, `a|b` one of the listed words.
- **Durations** are typed `90` (seconds), `45s`, `30m`, `2h`, `1d`, or combined like `1h30m`.
- **Colours** use `&` codes (`&c`, `&l`...) in every text a player sees.
- **Times of day** are written `"HH:mm"`, and dates `"yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm"`, in the file's `time-zone`.
